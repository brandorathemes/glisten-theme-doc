Billing & plans

Change your subscription plan

Review a plan change before you confirm it.

Sample article · September 5, 2026 · 1 min read

The short answer

Review features, limits, and the effective date in billing settings before changing your plan. Your product’s checkout should show the final cost and any proration.

Review your options

  1. Open Settings → Billing.
  2. Select Manage plan.
  3. Compare the available features and limits.
  4. Read the summary of charges and when the change takes effect.
  5. Confirm only after checking the impact on your workspace.

Before a downgrade

Check whether your workspace uses features or exceeds limits on the new plan. Export anything you need to keep and review the product’s retention policy.

Check the result

Return to billing settings to verify the plan and effective date. Save the confirmation for your records.

This is example content for a fictional product. Replace these steps with your product’s verified instructions before publishing.
